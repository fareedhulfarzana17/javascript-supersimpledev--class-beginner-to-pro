basically Math.floor will always take the minimum number from the decimal number(float) like for eg 2.9 supposeto be 3 but using Math.floor(2.9)=2

Math.round() its is normal rounding as we do in math

Math.floor() its a round down which means always take the minimum number

Math.ceil() its a round up which means always take the maximum number like (2.1=3)
